## Electronics and Wearable Use

CreepyEyes places hobby electronics, wiring, servos, batteries, and mechanical
components on or near the user's head and face.

You are solely responsible for determining whether your completed device is
safe and suitable to wear.

Before wearing or allowing another person to wear a CreepyEyes device:

- inspect all wiring for shorts, damaged insulation, exposed conductors, or
  loose solder joints;
- verify that circuit boards, connectors, switches, batteries, and other
  components are securely mounted;
- ensure that no sharp edges, fasteners, solder joints, wires, or mechanical
  parts can contact the user's eyes, face, skin, or hair;
- confirm that moving servo mechanisms cannot pinch, snag, strike, or trap
  the wearer;
- verify that the glasses can be removed immediately if necessary;
- test the completed device off-body before wearing it;
- stop using the device immediately if any component becomes unusually hot,
  damaged, loose, swollen, or behaves unexpectedly.

Do not wear or operate the device while driving, cycling, operating machinery,
or performing any activity where obscured vision, distraction, unexpected
movement, or equipment failure could create a hazard.

## Battery Safety

This project may use lithium-polymer (LiPo) batteries.

LiPo batteries can present fire, burn, electrical, and property-damage risks
if improperly charged, discharged, damaged, shorted, modified, punctured, or
stored.

Use only appropriate batteries and chargers. Follow the battery and charger
manufacturer's instructions and ratings.

Never use a swollen, punctured, overheated, damaged, or otherwise questionable
battery.

The repository owner and contributors do not warrant that any particular
battery, charger, wiring arrangement, boost converter, connector, or power
configuration is safe or suitable for your specific build.

## Electrical and Mechanical Variability

Component tolerances, servo behavior, wiring, soldering, battery condition,
boost-converter settings, mounting methods, glasses construction, and other
factors may differ significantly between builds.

Servo calibration values from one pair of glasses must not be assumed to be
correct for another pair.

Verify servo direction, open and closed endpoints, mechanical travel, current
draw, and physical clearance before normal use.

Do not allow a servo to continuously stall, bind, or force the eye mechanism.
